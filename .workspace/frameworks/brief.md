# Product research brief

Target product: Provided at runtime through the CLI input.

Research objective:
- Produce a comprehensive product research dossier for the product entered at startup.

Required coverage:
- Value proposition
- Target users and buyer personas
- Core user experience
- Features and workflows
- Pricing and business model
- Distribution and growth
- Competitive landscape and positioning
- Risks and limitations
- Moat and opportunities

Working rules:
- Distinguish facts, assumptions, and open questions.
- Prefer compact, evidence-aware conclusions.
- Turn long context into files instead of keeping everything in the prompt.
