# Product research rubric

Use this rubric to decide whether the research is comprehensive:

- Product: What job does the product do? What is the core workflow?
- Users: Who gets value first? Who pays? Who influences adoption?
- UX: Where is the onboarding friction? What creates delight or frustration?
- Business: How might pricing, retention, and expansion work?
- Competition: What are the closest alternatives and positioning gaps?
- Risk: What can block adoption, monetization, or trust?
- Opportunity: What seems underexploited but promising?
