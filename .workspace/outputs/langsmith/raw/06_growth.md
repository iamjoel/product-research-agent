# Langsmith Growth

## Questions
- What are the primary acquisition channels?
- Does growth depend on brand, channels, word of mouth, or product-led loops?
- Where is the growth bottleneck most likely to appear?

## Findings
Growth likely driven by developer adoption, integrations, and community engagement. Product-led growth is probable given the technical user base. Bottlenecks may include market awareness and onboarding complexity.

## Evidence Buckets
- Marketing channels
- User acquisition data
- Growth metrics
