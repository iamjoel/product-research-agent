# Langsmith Positioning

## Questions
- Who are the closest direct competitors?
- Where is the product differentiated, and where is it commoditized?
- If competitors copy the main selling points, what advantage remains?

## Findings
Langsmith's positioning is centered around providing a specialized platform for managing and optimizing language model workflows. It differentiates by focusing on developer experience and integration capabilities. Competitors include other AI workflow platforms and language model management tools.
