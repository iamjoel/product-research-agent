# Langsmith Risks

## Questions
- What is the biggest current product risk?
- Which capability gaps could hurt monetization or retention?
- What policy, technical, distribution, or organizational constraints matter?

## Findings
Risks include rapid competitor innovation, dependency on language model providers, and user churn due to complexity. Capability gaps in integrations or scalability could hurt retention. Policy risks may involve data privacy and compliance.

## Evidence Buckets
- Risk assessments
- User feedback
- Technical audits
